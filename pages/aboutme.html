An English teacher passionate about linguistics, dedicated to making language learning an insightful and enjoyable journey for everyone.

Hi! I'm Amir.

I have been teaching English as a Foreign or Second Language (EFL & ESL) at schools and language institutes since 2017. I hold an MA in Language Teaching, driven by the goal of becoming a truly professional educator. I live in Jovein, Iran, but I like to say I call many places 'home.' I am deeply passionate about cross-cultural communication and love learning about different cultural backgrounds. This curiosity is what fuels my free time, which I almost always spend studying linguistics. My favorite academic companion is The Study of Language by George Yule. If you’d like to chat, feel free to reach out—I am comfortable communicating in English, German, Turkish, and Persian. I would be thrilled to get to know you!

What is ESL Island?

As you might have guessed, ESL Island is a destination for language lovers. It is a space where we can study linguistics and have fun together. We share ideas, knowledge, and everything that helps us gain deeper insight into the amazing world of languages and cultures across the globe. Here, I will be sharing my notes from various books—especially my favorite, The Study of Language—as well as extra content regarding language teaching and learning. Your participation is what makes this island vibrant, so feel free to contribue!
