What is LunguFun?

As you may have already guessed, LinguFun is a combination of two words: Linguistics plus Fun. It stands for a place where we can study Linguistics and have fun together. We can also share ideas and knowledge. Anything that helps us gain more insight into this amazing field, languages, and cultures around the globe. I'll be sharing my notes from different books especially my favourite book "The Study of Language". Moreover, there will be extra content about teaching and learning languages. You can help me with that, as well.
