About Me

Hi! I'm Amir.

I have been teaching English as a Foreign or Second Language (EFL & ESL) at schools and language institutes since 2017. Currently I'm pursuing my MA in Language Teaching and want to become a professional teacher. I live in Jovein, Iran, but I call many places home. I love talking to people from different cultures and knowing more about their cultural backgrounds. Therefore, I always take advantage of my free times and study linguistics. My favourite book is "The Study of Language" by George Yule (1985). You may talk to me in English, German, Turkish, and Persian. I'll be glad to know you!
