Who am I?

Hi everyone!
Since you came to this page, you must've been interested in knowing more about me. So here we go:
My Name is Amir and I'm an English teacher/learner (EFL & ESL). I first dicovered my passion for learning different languages around the world when I was 17. It was truly an epiphany. I saw a conversation in German (Deutsch) and immediately relised I'm in love with this beautiful language. Yes, love at first sight. In order to learn it, first, I needed to know English because of lack of sufficient German learning materials in my mother tongue, Persian.
