About Me

Hi! I'm Amir.

I have been teaching English as a Foreign or Second Language (EFL & ESL) at schools and language institutes since 2017. Currently I'm pursuing my MA in Language Teaching and want to become a professional teacher. I live in Jovein, Iran, but I call many places home. I love talking to people from different cultures and knowing more about their cultural background. Therefore, I take advantage of my free times as much as possible and study linguistics. You may talk to me in English, German, Turkish, and Persian. I'll be glad to know you!
